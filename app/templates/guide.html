Quick start

From your rules to your first position

Ten short steps. Each one shows the real screen on a phone and takes you straight there. Your progress ticks off as you go.

{{ prog.count }} of {{ prog.total }} done
{{ head(1, 'signin', "You're signed in", None) }}

Your account was created the first time you signed in with Google, so there is nothing to fill in.

On iPhone? Open Profile and tap “Show me how” to add Tenetfolio to your Home Screen.
Open Profile
{{ shot('s1_login.jpg', 'The Tenetfolio log-in screen with a Continue with Google button') }}
{{ head(2, 'aikey', 'Add your AI key', 'More → AI settings') }}

Daily stories, Deep research and the assistant are written by an AI model. You bring the key, so you choose the model and the cost. Everything else works without it.

  • Pick a provider (Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter have free options) and paste its key.
  • The key is stored encrypted and is never shown again.
  • Optional: set a fallback model so a free-tier limit doesn't stop your stories.
Open AI settings
{{ shot('s_ai.jpg', 'AI settings with free provider options and the model and key fields') }}
{{ head(3, 'rules', 'Set your rules', 'More → Investment rules → My rules') }}

These are your own tests for a good business. Every stock page, the daily story and your portfolio are judged against them.

  • Rules are grouped: returns, balance-sheet safety, growth, cash and valuation, ownership.
  • Change a number or switch a rule off. A bar at the bottom saves all your changes at once.
  • A rule you have changed shows a small tag and a ↺ to put it back.
Start loose. You can tighten the numbers later and every list updates.
Open Investment rules
{{ shot('s2_rules.jpg', 'Investment rules with My rules, Screeners, Portfolio and Stock overrides tabs and grouped thresholds') }}
{{ head(4, 'myscreener', 'Make a screener', 'Investment rules → Screeners') }}

A screener is a named copy of your rules, for hunting in a different style without touching the rules your portfolio follows.

  • Name it, for example “Quality compounders”, and change only what differs, such as a stricter debt limit.
  • Use it in Analytics, on any stock page, or before a Deep research run.
  • On a tracked stock you can make it follow a screener until you switch it back.
Optional. Skip this until you have a second idea of what “good” means.
Open Screeners
{{ shot('s_screener.jpg', 'The Screeners tab with a screener card, Screen stocks and Edit rules buttons') }}
{{ head(5, 'find', 'Find stocks', 'Stocks tab') }}

Open Passes your rules to see only the stocks that clear the thresholds you set, or tap one of your own screeners (they carry a Mine tag).

  • Pick India, US or Both.
  • Prefer a ready-made idea? Try Steady compounders, Fast growers, Debt-free or Dividend payers.
  • Tap any row to open the stock.
Open the screens
{{ shot('s3_screen.jpg', 'Stocks tab with ready-made screens, a Mine-tagged screener and its matching stocks') }}
{{ head(6, 'analyse', 'Read a stock', None) }}

The page opens on Snapshot: one screen of pictures, built from filed numbers. See it against the market, against your rules, who owns it and whether profit turns into cash.

A strip shows how many of your rules it passes and names the ones it misses. Use the Screener picker there to judge the same stock against another set of rules.

  • Use the chips (Performance, Financial health, Valuation…) for the full tables.
  • Tap Edit rules if a miss tells you the rule, not the company, needs a rethink.
{{ shot('s4_stock.jpg', 'Titan Company stock page with price, revenue growth, operating margin and ROCE') }}
{{ head(7, 'track', 'Track it', None) }}

Choose Watchlist if you are only following it, then tap Track. A green “Tracking started” confirms it.

Already own it? Pick “Position” in the same menu instead.
{{ shot('s5_tracked.jpg', 'Titan page after tapping Track, showing the Tracking started confirmation') }}
{{ head(8, 'story', 'Check the story', 'Tracking tab → the stock') }}

Each tracked stock gets a plain-English story: what the latest numbers say, how it fares on your rules, and what needs your attention. It runs daily and each entry ends with the figures that would improve or weaken the read.

  • Tap Run analysis again to refresh it on demand.
  • Read the headline, then Needs your attention and the exit check.
  • Use Rules at the top to check this stock against one of your screeners instead.
In this example: “Watch — not actionable yet”. Nine rules pass, quick ratio doesn't, so keep watching.
Open Tracking
{{ shot('s6_story.jpg', 'Titan story showing a Watch verdict, rules summary and exit check') }}
{{ head(9, 'deep', 'Go deeper', 'Tracking tab → the stock → Deep research') }}

For a stock you care about, Deep research reads the latest earnings calls, investor deck and results filing, then writes a sourced report in about a minute.

  • Choose which rules to check it against, then press Generate.
  • Every claim cites a numbered source, every management quote is checked against the document, and the report says what it could not read.
  • Press Save as PDF to take it outside Tenetfolio. Your own rules and position are left out unless you tick the box.
Needs an AI key (step 2). You can start up to five reports a day.
Open Tracking
{{ shot('s_deep.jpg', 'The Deep research page with the Check it against picker, Save as PDF and the start of a report') }}
{{ head(10, 'position', 'Add your position', 'Story page → Journal tab') }}

Bought it? Log it in a few taps.

  • Choose Bought, the date, shares and price per share.
  • Add a one-line reason if you like. Your reasoning becomes part of the story.
  • Tap Add to journal. Quantity and average cost update straight away.
Many holdings? Ask the Assistant to import a broker statement (Excel, CSV or PDF). You review every line before anything is saved. Next time it reads “Bought more”, and the daily analysis flags where your actions fit, or break, your own rules.
Open Tracking
