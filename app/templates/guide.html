Quick start

From your rules to your first position

Seven short steps. Each one shows the real screen on a phone and takes you straight there. Your progress ticks off as you go.

{{ prog.count }} of {{ prog.total }} done
{{ head(1, 'signin', "You're signed in", None) }}

Your account was created the first time you signed in with Google, so there is nothing to fill in.

On iPhone? Open Profile and tap “Show me how” to add Tenetfolio to your Home Screen.
Open Profile
{{ shot('s1_login.jpg', 'The Tenetfolio log-in screen with a Continue with Google button') }}
{{ head(2, 'rules', 'Set your rules', 'More → Investment rules') }}

These are your own tests for a good business. Every screen and every stock page is judged against them.

  • Switch a rule on or off.
  • Set its threshold, for example ROCE above 20%, debt/equity below 2×, sales growth above 15%.
Start loose. You can tighten the numbers later and every list updates.
Open Investment rules
{{ shot('s2_rules.jpg', 'Investment rules list with ROCE, ROE, debt to equity, promoter pledge and sales growth thresholds') }}
{{ head(3, 'screener', 'Check the screener', 'Stocks tab') }}

Open Passes your rules to see only the stocks that clear the thresholds you just set.

  • Pick India, US or Both.
  • Prefer a ready-made idea? Try Steady compounders, Fast growers, Debt-free or Dividend payers.
  • Tap any row to open the stock.
Open the screener
{{ shot('s3_screen.jpg', 'Stocks tab with screens such as Passes your rules, Steady compounders and Fast growers, and a list of matching stocks') }}
{{ head(4, 'analyse', 'Analyse a stock', None) }}

The top of the page gives you price, latest sales growth, operating margin, ROCE and P/E against its own history.

Your rules then says how many pass and names the ones that don't. In this example Titan passes 9 of 10 and misses only the quick ratio.

  • Use the chips (Performance, Financial health, Valuation…) to go deeper.
  • Tap Edit rules if a miss tells you the rule, not the company, needs a rethink.
{{ shot('s4_stock.jpg', 'Titan Company stock page with price, revenue growth, operating margin, ROCE, P/E and a 9 of 10 rules summary') }}
{{ head(5, 'track', 'Add it to your tracker', None) }}

Choose Watchlist if you are only following it, then tap Track. A green “Tracking started” confirms it.

Already own it? Pick “Position” in the same menu instead.
{{ shot('s5_tracked.jpg', 'Titan page after tapping Track, showing the Tracking started confirmation') }}
{{ head(6, 'story', 'Check the story', 'Tracking tab → the stock') }}

Each tracked stock gets a plain-English story: what the latest numbers say, how it fares on your rules, and what needs your attention.

  • Tap Run analysis again to refresh it on demand. It also runs daily.
  • Read the headline, then Needs your attention and the exit check.
In this example: “Watch — not actionable yet”. Nine rules pass, quick ratio doesn't, so keep watching.
Open Tracking
{{ shot('s6_story.jpg', 'Titan story showing a Watch verdict, rules summary and exit check') }}
{{ head(7, 'position', 'Add your position', 'Story page → Journal tab') }}

Bought it? Log it in a few taps.

  • Choose Bought, the date, shares and price per share.
  • Add a one-line reason if you like. Your reasoning becomes part of the story.
  • Tap Add to journal. Quantity and average cost update straight away.
Next time it reads “Bought more”, and the daily analysis flags where your actions fit, or break, your own rules.
Open Tracking
