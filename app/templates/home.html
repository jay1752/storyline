A daily story for every stock you track

Add a position or a watchlist stock. Each morning an AI reads the new filings, the price move and the news against your thesis and writes a short update. Add a buy, a trim or a note and the next read takes it into account.

  • Rule-based flags from your own thresholds and sizing limits, plus the AI's own read
  • Optional journal: entries, exits and notes shape the story
  • Importing a broker statement PDF through chat fills in your positions

How it works

From a first look to a managed position.

  1. 1

    Research any stock

    Search a symbol or company name (⌘K from anywhere). Scan the quarterly matrix, open any chart full-size, and check cash flow, ownership and the balance sheet before you form a view.

  2. 2

    Test it against your rules

    Set your own bars for ROCE, debt, pledge, growth and margins, and how big a position may get. Every stock shows how many of your rules it passes.

  3. 3

    Track it and manage it

    Add it to your watchlist or positions. Each morning Stocktale tells you what needs attention: trim, hold or exit, sized by your limits.
