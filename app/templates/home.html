Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. Tenetfolio applies your own rules to public data and writes a sourced reading. It gives no price targets and no instruction to buy or sell. Check the figures against the filings before you act.

Where does the data come from?

Company filings and exchange data (NSE and SEC), shareholding filings and market data providers. Every report lists its sources with dates. Figures can contain errors, so check them against the filings before you act.

What does a report not read?

The annual report is not read yet, and a scanned document with no text cannot be read. The report says exactly which documents it used.

Does it replace my own thinking?

It replaces the reading, not the judgement. You still decide what the numbers mean for you.
