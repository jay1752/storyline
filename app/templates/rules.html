Investment rules

The thresholds behind the Fundamentals scan on every stock's story page, which of your stocks get a story automatically each day, and how you size positions. They apply in three layers: the platform default, your defaults, and an override for a single stock. Anything you don't change inherits from the layer above, and ↺ Reset sends it back.

{% if start %}

Set your rules in two steps

  1. Thresholds: what a good stock means to you. This drives the “Passes your rules” screen in Analytics and every stock’s verdict.
  2. Position sizing: how big a holding may grow, when to trim, and your sector limit. This drives the alerts on your tracked stocks.

Defaults are already filled in. Change a number, then press Save on that row. Anything you leave stays at the default.
